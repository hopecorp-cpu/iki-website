Help keep this a respectful space.

Respect people and protect privacy

Do not criticise appearances, judge or compare results. Do not share another person’s photos, medical records, phone number or messages without permission.

Share experiences responsibly

Distinguish personal experience from sourced information. Do not advise others to stop medication or change treatment on their own.

No spam or misleading solicitation

Do not send bulk advertising, solicit members to other channels or impersonate the IKI team.

Need help or want to report something?

Send the link to the content you wish to report to contact@ikihealing.com or IKI on Zalo. Share only what is needed for the team to review it.
