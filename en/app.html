You may be wondering

Has the app launched?

IKI Beauty is being designed and developed. The web app comes first, with iOS and Android planned for a later stage.

Can AI replace a doctor?

No. AI assists with information and tasks; medical concerns require a qualified professional.

How will photos and personal information be handled?

Processing, storage locations, retention and deletion must be documented for the actual system before launch. Design images are not a commitment to a particular data architecture.
