Movement

3-day content

Reference outline of the programme's content.

Day 1 · Laying the knowledge foundation

Review your current lifestyle, understand the Body – Mind – Spirit care approach, and choose what you want to change.

Day 2 · Lifestyle practice

Learn about meals, breathing, rest and gentle movement through guidance content from IKI Academy.

Day 3 · Long-term integration

Discuss difficulties in applying what you've learned; choose practice steps that fit your work and family.

Who is this programme for?

You want to start with lifestyle

Suitable if you are interested in meals, rest rhythms and self-care, but are not sure where to start.

Women are the group IKI focuses on supporting; family members may also look into the programme.

Talk to your doctor before joining

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, or have a health condition that needs monitoring, talk to your doctor about whether you can join and about the practice content.

The course shares lifestyle knowledge and does not replace medical consultation, diagnosis or treatment.
